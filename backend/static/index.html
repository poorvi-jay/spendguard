Guardrails for AI agents

Let agents shop. Without an unchecked wallet.

The problem: an AI agent connected to a payment tool can spend whatever it decides to. It can misread a request, ignore a budget, or be tricked by a product page. SpendGuard sits between the agent and your money: every PayPal payment is checked against your budget, your rules and what you actually asked for, before any money moves.

How it works

The agent shops. SpendGuard decides whether money moves.

01

Your request is pinned

What you asked for is stored on the server before shopping starts. The agent can't rewrite it later, even if a product page tells it to.

02

Rules run first

Monthly budget, per-purchase cap and blocked categories run at checkout. Over the cap? SpendGuard nudges to a cheaper option instead.

03

PayPal holds, then SpendGuard decides

You approve in PayPal, which only authorizes. An AI check compares the item to your request, then SpendGuard captures or voids.
